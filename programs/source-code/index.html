Programs/Source Code Accelerator

Source Code
Accelerator

A 7-week online program for software developers in Iraq. AI can now produce a lot of code, but it still cannot beat human ingenuity, empathy, ethical judgement and creativity. That part is your job, and this program will teach you to do it well.

Apply now See what you’ll learn
Dates
20 Nov 2026 – 23 Jan 2027
Format
7 weeks online, with a weekly live session on Zoom
Who it’s for
Software developers in Iraq, aged 18–35
Supported by
ProDIGI, funded by the German Government and co-funded by the European Union, with GIZ

Most AI courses sell the hype. Source Code Accelerator is not your typical course. From the first week to the final project, you’ll be pushed to question how you think, weigh the promises of AI against the evidence, take its risks seriously, and still ask how it can be put to work for people and communities.

Topics covered

Seven weeks, built around how developers work now

First half · Human skills

Skills and practices that make you a more responsive, present and reliable team member.

  1. 01

    Presence, Purpose and the World Ahead

  2. 02

    Writing, Feedback and Working with Others

  3. 03

    Problem Framing and Systems Thinking

  4. 04

    AI Risks and Ethics

Second half · Hands-on with AI

Leveraging AI as a developer, ending with a final project that simulates a real work scenario.

  1. 05

    AI for Good and Open Models

  2. 06

    Agentic Development

  3. 07

    Code Review, Trust and Security

  4. 08

    Building and Evaluating with Models

Topics may change at the organisers’ discretion.

Expect to change your mind.

Over the program, you will
  • Reflect on what you want from your work as AI reshapes the field
  • Sharpen the skills that matter most in the age of AI: clear writing, useful feedback, thinking in systems and framing problems well
  • Examine how AI fails, and how it can be built for good
  • Work hands-on with agentic coding tools and models, and learn to review what they produce
  • Join weekly live sessions and learn alongside developers from across Iraq
By graduation, you’ll walk away with
  • A clear view of where AI helps your work, and where it gets in the way
  • Habits for reviewing, testing and securing AI-generated code
  • The ability to evaluate a model before you trust it
  • A final project that simulates a real workplace scenario requiring skill, collaboration and analytical thinking
