How we measure

What counts, and how we check

  1. What counts as a placement

    Paid employment, freelance work or further study in a relevant field within six months of graduating.

  2. When we check

    We survey graduates at graduation, then again at three and six months.

  3. How we verify

    Self-reported outcomes are checked against employer confirmation or public profiles where possible.

  4. What we publish

    Figures for every cohort, including the ones that fell short, with notes on why.
